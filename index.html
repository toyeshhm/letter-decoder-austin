    Why we built this

    Nobody should lose a home over a letter they couldn't read.

    Austin is home to families from all over the world, and to neighbors who've lived here for 80 years. The letters they get are written for lawyers. A missed date can mean an eviction, a shutoff or a lost case.

    Letter Decoder is the friend at the kitchen table who reads it with you, and knows who to call.
